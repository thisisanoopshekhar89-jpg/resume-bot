How it works

  1. Paste a JD or upload a screenshot; optionally add the company & role.
  2. The bot scans for domain keywords, floats matching skills to the top, re-ranks each job's bullets and projects, and rebuilds your headline.
  3. Download the regenerated résumé and cover letter, and review the match & gap report that appears right here.

Tip: launch with Start Resume Bot.bat (or python app.py). Don't open index.html directly — it must run through the app.
